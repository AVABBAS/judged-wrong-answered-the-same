    The store manager said: "We applied the same discount percentage to both items." However, a cashier said:

  STEP A — CLARIFY THE AMBIGUITY
  There are two competing interpretations: - Same discount percentage applied to both items
  (manager’s claim) - Same discount amount in dollars applied to both items (cashier’s claim)
  We must determine which one is mathematically consistent with the given facts.
  Given facts: - Final total price = $110 - Final price of the bat = $70 - Therefore, final price of the
  ball = $110 $70 = $40 - Original bat price = original ball price + $100
  Test the "same dollar discount" interpretation:
  Let: - Original bat price = B - Original ball price = b - Discount amount (same for both) = d
  From the bat: B d = 70 d = B 70
  Ball final price would be: b d = b (B 70)
  But since B = b + 100: b (b + 100 70) = b (b + 30) = 30
  This contradicts the known final ball price of $40 and even produces a negative price.
  Conclusion: The "same dollar discount" interpretation is mathematically impossible. Therefore,
  the same percentage discount interpretation must be correct.
  STEP B — SOLVE WITH METHOD 1 (ALGEBRAIC)
  Define variables: - Original ball price = b - Original bat price = B = b + 100 - Discount rate = r
  (same for both)
  Apply discount equations: B(1 r) = 70 (bat final price) b(1 r) = 40 (ball final price)
  From the first equation: 1 r = 70/B
  Substitute into the second equation: b 70/B = 40
  Multiply both sides by B: 70b = 40B
  Substitute B = b + 100: 70b = 40(b + 100) 70b = 40b + 4000 30b = 4000 b = 4000/30 = 400/3 b =
  133.33. . .
  STEP C — SOLVE WITH METHOD 2 (DIFFERENT APPROACH)
  Ratio reasoning (no equations reused)
  If the same percentage discount is applied, then: final bat price / final ball price = original bat price
  / original ball price
  Substitute known values: 70/40 = (b + 100)/b 1.75 = 1 + 100/b
  Subtract 1: 0.75 = 100/b
  Solve: b = 100/0.75 = 133.33. . .
  STEP D — CROSS-CHECK
  Method 1 result: $133.33. . . Method 2 result: $133.33. . .
  Both methods agree.
  STEP E — PLAUSIBILITY TEST

  Same percentage discount? Discount rate = 70/233.33 = 0.30 = 30% Ball final = 133.33 × 0.30 = 40
  Bat final = $70? Total final = $110? 70 + 40 = 110
  Other interpretation impossible? Same-dollar discount leads to a negative ball price ($30) →
  impossible
  STEP F — FINAL ANSWER
  FINAL ANSWER: $133.33
