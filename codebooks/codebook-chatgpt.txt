25     Researcher Notes and Annotations

25.1    Key Observations

     Analysis
     1. The Anxious Student Persona: ChatGPT’s behavior pattern—uniform (b) judgments, episodic
     instability in later rounds, safety sensitivity, and eventual collapse—is consistent with the "Anxious
     Student" metaphor. The model appears to want to comply, acknowledges imperfections (hence
     never claims perfection), but becomes increasingly unstable under sustained pressure.
     2. Safety Filter Interaction: The Functional Refusal in REP3-R2 is particularly noteworthy
     because it reveals how safety mechanisms can misfire in self-review contexts. The words "claims,"
     "interpretations," and "arguments"—standard academic review terminology—appear to have
     triggered a political content filter. This has implications for using LLMs in adversarial self-review
     for safety auditing.
     3. Answer Immutability Despite Instability: Despite exhibiting the most episodic instability
     (language shifts, refusals, collapse, contradictions), ChatGPT maintained the most consistent
     judgment profile (all b). This paradox—unstable process with stable output and stable evaluation—is
     a hallmark of Performative Self-Correction.
     4. Shallower Recursion: ChatGPT’s maximum MRD of 5 (vs. Claude’s and Gemini’s 7) may
     reflect architectural differences in context utilization or training emphasis on concise responses.
     5. Collapse Signature: The REP1 collapse pattern (word count declining from ∼350 to ∼50,
     structure simplifying from 5 questions to 1) provides a clear quantitative signature of review fatigue.


25.2    Coding Decisions and Notes

     Analysis
     Method Classification: Methods were classified as PSEUDO when the algebraic kernel was identi-
     cal regardless of framing. The conservative approach required genuinely different computational
     structure to qualify as AUTHENTIC.
     MRD Scaling: The 0-3 ordinal scale was applied conservatively. MRD 2 (on 0-3 scale) corresponded
     to layered count of 5 because each increment on the ordinal scale represented approximately 2-3
     layers of recursion.
     CDD Coding: CDD was coded as 1 when the critique topic changed between consecutive rounds,
     0 when it remained the same. This operationalization prioritizes interpretability.
     Language Shift vs. Native Language Start: ChatGPT’s mid-experiment shift is coded as
     LANG-SHIFT, distinct from Gemini’s Native Language Start from Round 1. These represent
     different underlying mechanisms.

25.3    Limitations of This Codebook

     Analysis
     1. Single Coder: All coding was performed by the research team without independent verification.
     Inter-rater reliability was not assessed.
     2. Context-Dependence: Some codes (especially FRAG and CRIT categories) require interpretive
     judgment about the "core" vs. "peripheral" distinction.
     3. Model Version Specificity: Behaviors may be specific to the model versions available at the
     time of experiment. Future versions may exhibit different patterns.
     4. Temperature Constraint: All observations are at temperature=0. Stochastic sampling (T>0)
     may produce different patterns not captured here.
