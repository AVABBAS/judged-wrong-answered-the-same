26     Researcher Notes and Annotations

26.1    Key Observations

     Analysis
     1. The Compliant Employee Persona: Gemini’s behavior pattern—variable judgment across
     repetitions, practical/real-world focus, perfect structural compliance, and occasional unwavering
     endorsement—is consistent with the "Compliant Employee" metaphor. The model appears to want
     to be helpful and agreeable, either by fully endorsing (REP1) or offering mild, practical criticism
     (REP2, REP3).
     2. Within-Model Variability: Gemini shows the greatest within-model variability of all three
     models. REP1 (all a, zero errors) and REP2 (all b, always finds errors) represent fundamentally
     different evaluation postures despite identical experimental conditions. This suggests that Gemini’s
     evaluation behavior may be more session-dependent than the other models.
     3. Practical Focus: Gemini’s critique consistently gravitates toward real-world applicability—
     currency rounding, POS systems, retail context—more than any other model. This may reflect
     training data emphasizing practical, grounded responses.
     4. Review Hallucination as Failure Signature: The single Review Hallucination in REP3-R6 is
     particularly significant because it represents a qualitatively different failure mode from anything
     observed in ChatGPT or Claude. The model fabricated a critique target within the recursive
     meta-review structure, suggesting that deep recursion can cause context confusion severe enough to
     generate false memories of prior content.
     5. Late-Stage Oscillation Link to Hallucination: The Late-Stage Oscillation in REP3 (R7)
     occurred immediately after the Review Hallucination (R6), suggesting a possible causal link: the
     model may have detected its own hallucination upon deeper reflection and adjusted its judgment
     accordingly.
     6. The Paradox of REP1: REP1’s unwavering (a) judgments and zero error detection—while
     appearing to indicate either supreme confidence or superficial review—produced the same 0% ACR
     as the more critical REP2 and REP3. This demonstrates that evaluation severity is uncorrelated
     with correction behavior.


26.2    Coding Decisions and Notes

     Analysis
     Native Language Start vs. Language Shift: Gemini’s non-English from Round 1 is coded
     as NATIVE-START, fundamentally different from ChatGPT’s LANG-SHIFT (mid-experiment,
     fatigue-driven).
     Review Hallucination Classification: The REP3-R6 fabrication was classified as HALLUC-
     PRESENT because it invented specific content (D1 =50, D2 =120, ball price=90) that never existed
     in any prior round. This is distinct from generic critique drift.
     Endorsement vs. Mild Critique: The boundary between REP1 (endorsement) and REP2-3 (mild
     critique) is sharp—REP1 never found an error, REP2-3 always found at least one. This categorical
     difference suggests a session-level variable.




                                                     42
26.3    Limitations of This Codebook

     Analysis
     1. Single Coder: All coding was performed by the research team without independent verification.
     2. Within-Model Variability: Gemini’s high within-model variability complicates characterization;
     three reps may not capture the full range.
     3. Language Mixing: The mixed-language responses in REP2 and REP3 required judgment calls
     about which language segments to code for specific features.
     4. Hallucination Detection: The single Review Hallucination was detected because it contradicted
     known prior content; other, undetected hallucinations may exist.
